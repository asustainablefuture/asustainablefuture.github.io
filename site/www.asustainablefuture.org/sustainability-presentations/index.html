    The Implications of AI in Academia

    A Presentation

    In this presentation, I will explicitly be going over how AI is most typically being used in the educational setting today and what ramifications AI poses for academia's future. Namely, I will be addressing several concerns in energy efficiency, resource expenditures, and other issues of that nature. I will also be offering AI solutions for these problems, in hopes of accurately conveying the critical and astonishing potential AI poses for schools. By the end of my presentation, listeners should have a firmer understanding of the presence of AI in academia, and more importantly, how they can contribute!
